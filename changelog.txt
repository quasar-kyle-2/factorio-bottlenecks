Version: 1.1.2
Date: 2026-11-26
  Features:
    - Project recommendations: a new pane lists the 10 recipes you don't use yet that would save
      the most build cost (one-time effort) at the speeds you chose, each built fresh in a building
      type you have or one your factory makes (an assembler set to make quenching tower MK02s means
      MK02s are tried): how many to build, how much build cost and running cost they save (as bars,
      the numbers in the tooltips), and which new buildings they make unnecessary; a button in the
      pane's title bar shows how many, and which ones they need more of. They are
      slow to work out, so only when you press "Recommend projects" in the Items pane; closing the
      pane pauses the search, and finished recommendations stay until the next Refresh. The item
      report's Productivity section waits while they are worked out
    - Items: a short item better handled by one of those projects gets a red "!", its tooltip
      naming the project
    - Item report: an "add" column in Goes to and Made by shows the new buildings the plan adds to
      each group, and the summary shows how many it adds for the item
    - Item report: a building group's tooltip lists its current effects: its modules and the
      beacons on it
    - Refresh is much faster after small changes: when you've added or removed buildings, changed
      their modules or recipes, or placed ghosts, it carries on from the last answer instead of
      working everything out again, with the same result (on a large Pyanodon's base, about 10
      seconds instead of a minute and a half, most of it the scan). It still starts from scratch
      after research or a change to your unlocked recipes (Pyanodon's TURD choices included), a
      change to mods or mod settings, unlimited items or targets, when the factory needs an item it
      didn't before, or when a few hundred building groups changed at once. If carrying on takes
      more than a quarter of the work a fresh start took last time, it starts over. "Recommend
      projects" after such a Refresh also skips its first step, looking through every recipe you
      could build, unless you've built a type of building you didn't have or started or stopped
      using a recipe (on a large Pyanodon's base, about 4 seconds sooner)
  Changes:
    - The analysis pauses while the window is closed and carries on when you open it again,
      instead of slowing the game down in the background
    - Extra buildings are planned by build cost: one more building of a group you have costs less than
      a new one, since its belts and pipes are already there
    - Build cost counts belt lanes: an item one lane of your best belt can't carry pays half for
      each extra lane. It also counts power: 0.05 per MW a new building draws, for the power plant
      behind it
    - Between plans of about the same build cost, the one with the lowest running cost wins, instead of
      the one that uses the smallest share of each group of your buildings
    - Steam, water at other than its default temperature and waste that recipes make are counted
      like any other item. The "Track steam" setting is gone
    - Fuels made for one kind of building (drill heads, fuel rods) are counted as ingredients
    - Items that come from outside the factory (nexelit ore from dig sites, deliveries, what you
      hand-feed) are counted, capped at what came in during the last hour
    - Item report: in Made by, building groups left below 100% while another group making the
      item runs past 100% get an info icon by "needed", explaining why they sit idle
  Bugfixes:
    - While the analysis runs, the window no longer stretches to the bottom of the screen, and the
      Targets pane lines up with the edges

Version: 0.2.2
Date: 2026-11-20
  Features:
    - Item report: click an item for where it goes and where it comes from, in its own pane under
      the targets. Every building using it (and how many of them are waiting for it right now), every
      building making it (what else it makes, which of its own inputs are short, what it is doing
      now), and what the item mostly becomes on the way to your targets. Click any item icon in
      the report for that item's report
    - Item report: the slot at the top takes an item or fluid (or its ghost) dropped on it, or click
      it to pick one, and shows that report. With nothing shown, the slot is there to start one
    - "Add consumers" takes fluids and ghosts too
    - Item report: "Goes to" shows what each building makes with the item, shortest first, and the
      way to your targets follows a short product where there is one (that is what the building is
      pushed for)
    - Click a building icon in the item report to show those buildings on the map (each click the
      next one), with green boxes around all of them for 12 seconds
    - A stop button next to the progress cancels the analysis (and the lag). The targets, speeds
      and unlimited items stay; Refresh starts again
  Changes:
    - Compact (pinned) view: shows the Items pane with only the difference column (20 short items,
      then how many more; Enough as two counts: balanced and surplus), "Add consumers", the targets'
      speeds without sliders, and a slimmer item report (just the difference at the top, no live
      status, only short products, one difference column for what buildings make, a narrower path)
    - The rescan button says "Refresh" next to its icon
    - The Targets pane no longer repeats "Targets" inside it: its info icon is in the pane title
    - The mod's icon is sky blue instead of dark blue
    - The Items and Item report panes are hidden while there are no results
    - The targets show while working it out (read-only until it finishes or is cancelled) and
      after a cancel, so they can be removed or their speeds changed without waiting for it
    - Removing targets, or adding to the ones there are, waits for Apply instead of scanning and
      working it out right away, so several changes cost one analysis. Removed groups show 0 in
      yellow (click again to bring them back); added buildings show as "+N buildings to add".
      The first targets on a clean slate still start right away. The button left of Apply undoes
      all of it (speeds too)
    - Add consumers always adds every building on the surface that uses the item, whatever the
      targets are. It used to skip the ones that don't feed the targets, which depended on the
      last results (none after a cancel, out of date while target changes wait for Apply)
    - While working it out, the selection tool button and Add consumers are hidden, and dragging
      the selection tool or right-clicking an item for unlimited does nothing: cancel or wait
      first. Refresh and Reset still start over
    - Item report: the difference replaces the coverage at the top
    - Ash is no longer hard-coded as unlimited: any item a fuel leaves behind when burnt, that no
      recipe refills one for one into that fuel, counts as waste (ash, spent nuclear fuel cells).
      Empty canisters, used batteries and used animals stay counted
    - Item icons: click opens the item report, right-click switches unlimited on and off
    - Fluid icons can be pipetted too, like items
    - The report uses the buildings you already have first, and only plans past them where it
      has to. Needs now grow steadily as you turn a target up, instead of jumping between
      recipes from one speed to the next
    - Where it has to go past your buildings, it prices each extra building by what it takes to
      fit in: its width, one per item or fluid it uses or makes, +1 for heat, +2 for burnt fuel
      (+1 for fluid fuel), whatever you already have of it
    - Items needed at a negligible rate (solver crumbs) are no longer listed
    - Speed sliders snap to 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100, 150 and 200%. Moving one no longer
      starts working right away: the new speed shows in yellow until you press Apply
    - The difference has its own column, and is hidden when it is basically 0
    - Coverage colors: yellow below 105% (and for short items that round to 100%), green from 105%
    - Enough lists the items up to 125% coverage, then skips the surplus ones but the 20 most
      over-covered
    - The Apply button hides once the new speeds are worked out; the window is taller
    - Item columns line up across tables; the most over-covered items get their own column headers
    - With nothing short, Short (0) says so, and Enough has no empty header row
    - The Targets pane fills its frame when empty
    - The progress percentage follows the solve more closely instead of sitting at 99%
    - Items made by a show-only recipe (hidden, out of nothing) are treated as unlimited: Pyanodon's
      dig sites claim 4000 nexelit ore per second, while their digosaurs really mine it by script
  Bugfixes:
    - No blank first line in item icon tooltips

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-19
  Major Features:
    - New report: for every item your targets' supply chain uses, how much is needed and how much
      your factory can make, and the coverage (can make / needed). Short items first, lowest
      coverage first: the order things run out in as your targets speed up. Each item is counted
      as if everything it needs were there, so a shortage upstream never hides what is needed
      further down; only what the targets need counts (sinks, buildables and other consumers
      add nothing)
    - A speed for each target group (slider, 0-200%): the report is worked out for those speeds.
      Changing one continues from the last answer (under a second on a large base)
  Changes:
    - Ash is treated as unlimited, like water: getting rid of it isn't a real use
    - Removed: bottlenecks, limiting items, must-run targets, build plans and "what it takes"
    - Clicking an item switches it between counted and unlimited
